| Records under our control | Retention and deletion rule |
| --- | --- |
| Account and current Discord link | While needed for the account. On a verified closure or deletion request, stop unnecessary processing promptly and aim to complete ordinary live-record deletion within 7 calendar days, subject to required blocking or legal retention. We review accounts annually to identify records no longer needed. |
| OAuth state and routine technical records | OAuth state expires after 10 minutes; consumed/expired state is cleared within 7 days. Rate-limit records, routine error records and superseded check details are kept no longer than 30 days. | Ordinary support | While the request is open, then no more than 90 days after closure, including staff Discord copies and our email copies. We review open cases weekly. Earlier justified deletion requests are addressed promptly. |
| Terms, age and minimal case-completion evidence | Only customer ID, version, timestamp, adult declaration and minimal record of the action taken, during the account and for up to 12 months after closure unless a specific legal requirement determines otherwise. ## 6. ARCO rights

You may independently request access to your data and processing information, rectification, cancellation or opposition by writing to **sales@aztlan-eng.com** or the controller's address. Active membership is not required, and you do not need to explain a reason for requesting access.

Include your name, a reply channel, the right or request, the data concerned where applicable, and information that helps locate them, such as your account email or customer ID if known. Verify your identity and, for a representative, their identity and authority. For rectification, describe and substantiate the correction. We will provide a proportionate, restricted identity-verification method; do not publicly post documents or send passwords or codes. Not knowing your customer ID does not prevent a request.

We will communicate a determination within **20 business days** of receipt and, if granted, implement it within **15 business days** after communicating the response. Each period may be extended once for an equal period if justified, with the reason communicated to you. We will explain any obstacle, refusal or partial fulfillment. After identity verification, data will be delivered through an agreed secure electronic channel; the process and electronic delivery will be free.

If dissatisfied or unanswered, you may approach the **Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno**, the authority identified in Mexico's private-sector data protection law, through its current channels. Legal filing deadlines apply to rights-protection requests: generally 15 business days after the response; if no response is given, a request may be filed after the controller's response period expires. You need not exhaust informal support reviews to pursue legal remedies. [LFPDPPP, articles 27–40, Spanish official text](https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf/LFPDPPP.pdf).
